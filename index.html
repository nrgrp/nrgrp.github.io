Neurorobotics Group

Department of Computer Science, University of Freiburg
 

Welcome to the Neurorobotics Group at the University of Freiburg!

We are part of the Department of Computer Science, as well as the Cluster of Excellence BrainLinks-BrainTools. We develop machine learning techniques for robotics and neurotechnology. We focus on data-efficient deep reinforcement learning methods that provide robust and adaptive solutions, and target applications on real-world devices.
